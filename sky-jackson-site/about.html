About

I'm Sky Jackson. Digital Pour-Over is where I work out what happened to my attention — how devices that once felt like magic quietly became appliances, and what it takes to notice them again. The coffee metaphor isn't decoration. I learned to slow down over a pour-over first, and then went looking for the same thing everywhere else.

By day I'm the Communications Coordinator and photographer for New Mexico's Early Childhood Education and Care Department, which means I spend my weeks trying to say complicated things clearly to families, providers, and legislators — three audiences who share almost no vocabulary. Most of what I've learned about writing, I learned from watching those messages fail and figuring out why.

I studied philosophy at UNM on the pre-law track, which is a long way of saying I spent four years being told, in writing, that my sentences did not mean what I thought they meant. That turned out to be the job. Before this I taught digital media arts, ran recruitment and outreach for a nonprofit, managed social accounts, and freelanced — shooting, writing, and designing for whoever needed it that month. On paper it reads as scattered. In practice it was the same work every time: find out what somebody actually needs to know, then get it to them before their attention runs out.

Those two things are less separate than they look. The job is about holding somebody's attention long enough to hand them something useful. The blog is about noticing what happened to my own. Expect posts on the tools I've torn apart and rebuilt, the ones I abandoned, and a running account of whatever I am currently getting wrong.
